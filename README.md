# Little Panda Space

A night-sky, mobile-first astronomy site for 5–7 year olds. It's the third app in the family, after [Little Panda Maths](https://github.com/pjanak/little-panda-maths) and [Little Panda Science](https://github.com/pjanak/little-panda-science): Bao the panda guides children through eight games, and every question is read aloud, including the answer choices.

Space isn't in the Key Stage 1 curriculum for England ("Earth and space" is taught in Year 5), so these games introduce the big ideas early and link to the KS1 topics on seasons, day length and light.

## Games

**Earth, Sun and Moon**

- **Day and Night**: the side of the Earth facing the Sun has day. On Medium, children spin the Earth themselves until it's day or night for Bao.
- **Moon Shapes**: crescent, half, gibbous, full and new moon, then putting the phases in order.
- **Our Star, the Sun**: which things make their own light, then Sun facts and sun safety.
- **Space Explorers**: rockets, astronauts, satellites and telescopes, then famous firsts (Gagarin, Armstrong, Laika, Tim Peake).

**Planets and stars**

- **Meet the Planets**: recognise each planet from a picture or a clue, then planet facts.
- **Planet Line-up**: put the planets in order from the Sun, building up to all eight ("My Very Easy Method Just Speeds Up Naming").
- **Big and Small**: everything is drawn the same size, so children must know which is really bigger.
- **Star Pictures**: join the stars to draw constellations (Cassiopeia, the Plough, Orion and more), then name them by shape.

## Difficulty levels

| Game | Easy | Medium | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day and Night | Is it day or night for Bao? | Spin the Earth to make it day or night | Days, years, sunrise and sunset |
| Moon Shapes | Crescent, half or full | Find the picture, adding new and gibbous | Put the phases in order, and why the Moon changes |
| Our Star, the Sun | Does it make its own light? | Sun facts and safety | Distance, what it's made of, why it seems to move |
| Space Explorers | Name the rocket, astronaut, satellite or telescope | What would you use for each job? | Famous space firsts |
| Meet the Planets | Name the planet from its picture | Tap the planet from a clue | Planet facts |
| Planet Line-up | 3 planets next to each other | 4 planets from anywhere | All 8 planets |
| Big and Small | Which of two is really bigger? | Order three from smallest to biggest | Size facts |
| Star Pictures | Join numbered stars (small shapes) | Follow the twinkling star (bigger shapes) | Name the constellation |

Right answers earn stars and confetti, and every 5 stars brings up a celebration.

## Grown-up settings

Open **Grown-up settings** at the bottom of the home screen:

- **Play timer**: pick 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes. A countdown shows in the top bar, turns red with a spoken warning at one minute, and then Bao says "Time's up!" and shows the stars earned in that session. Holding the button for 2 seconds adds 5 more minutes.
- **Reset stars**: sets the star count back to 0, after a confirmation step.

## Running it

It's a single static file with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder on any static host (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify).
